### Country of Survey Takers
- **Visualization**: A treemap showcasing the distribution of survey respondents by country.  
- **Highlights**: Major responses from the United States, India, United Kingdom, and Canada.

---

### Favorite Programming Languages
- **Visualization**: A bar chart displaying the most preferred programming languages among survey respondents.  
- **Highlights**: Python ranks as the most popular language across job titles.

---

### Average Salary by Job Title
- **Visualization**: A horizontal bar chart showing the average salary for various job titles.  
- **Highlights**: Data Scientists have the highest average salary compared to other job roles.

---

### Count of Survey Takers and Average Age
- **Statistics**:  
  - **Number of survey respondents**: 630  
  - **Average age**: 29.87 years  

---

### Difficulty to Break into Data Science
- **Visualization**: A donut chart showing the perceived difficulty of entering the data science field.  
- **Highlights**: The majority of respondents rated it as "Neither easy nor difficult."

---

### Average Salary and Work-Life Balance
- **Visualization**: A gauge chart showing the work-life balance rating.  
- **Highlights**: The average work-life balance rating is **5.74** out of 10.

---

### Happy with their Salary
- **Visualization**: A gauge chart representing salary satisfaction.  
- **Highlights**: The average salary satisfaction rating is **4.27** out of 10.
